## 📋 Overview

This project applies a **dual-lens framework** to investment analysis by combining:

| Lens | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 📊 Lens 1 | Quantitative financial performance data |
| 🧠 Lens 2 | Qualitative analysis of AI initiatives and strategic documents |

The goal is to create a more complete picture of organizational potential by evaluating both current market performance and future-facing innovation signals — giving analysts a richer foundation for investment decisions than financial data alone can provide.

---

## 🚀 What It Does

🏦 **Analyzes financial metrics across companies**
Pulls and evaluates structured financial performance data to assess current market standing.

📄 **Ingests and processes PDF-based AI initiative documents**
Reads strategic documents and AI initiative reports to extract qualitative signals about organizational direction.

🔎 **Uses chunking and vector retrieval to query strategic information**
Splits documents into semantically meaningful chunks and stores them in a vector database for intelligent retrieval.

🧩 **Combines structured financial and qualitative data into a unified analysis workflow**
Merges both lenses into a single output that gives a complete view of an organization from both a performance and innovation perspective.
